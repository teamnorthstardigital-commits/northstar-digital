01. Website Design

Website design is the process of planning how a new website looks, communicates and guides its visitors. It includes visual direction, page structure, typography and interaction concepts.

  • Visual direction and layout
  • Page hierarchy and structure
  • Brand-aligned presentation
  • Content placement
  • Responsive design planning

Useful when: a business needs a new digital presence or wants to introduce a new product or service.

02. Website Redesign

A website redesign examines an existing website and explores how its presentation, structure and usability might be improved.

  • Visual refresh
  • Content organisation
  • Navigation improvements
  • Responsive refinements
  • Updated page layouts

Useful when: a website no longer reflects the business or its information is difficult to explore.

03. Responsive Design

Responsive design considers how website layouts adapt to different screen sizes and devices.

  • Mobile layout planning
  • Tablet and desktop layouts
  • Readable typography
  • Flexible content sections
  • Touch-friendly interactions

Purpose: make the website comfortable to use across different devices.

04. UI and UX Thinking

UI refers to the visual interface. UX considers the overall experience people have when navigating and using the website.

  • Navigation planning
  • Page hierarchy
  • Interaction concepts
  • Information organisation
  • Content clarity

Purpose: make information easier to find and actions easier to understand.

05. Digital Experiences

Digital experiences bring together visual storytelling, movement, interactions and content to create a distinctive website presentation.

  • Motion concepts
  • Scroll-based animation ideas
  • Interactive components
  • Visual storytelling
  • Distinctive presentation

Purpose: create a more engaging and memorable digital presentation without sacrificing clarity.

06. AI-Powered Concepts

AI-powered website concepts explore how artificial intelligence might support visitors and improve access to information.

  • AI assistant concepts
  • Conversational interfaces
  • Content discovery ideas
  • Automation planning
  • Backend integration planning

Important: a live AI feature requires a secure backend, an AI provider and appropriate configuration.

What happens before a project begins?

Our process

A structured approach helps make responsibilities, decisions and project expectations easier to understand.

Insights and resources

Understand the digital world more clearly.

Educational information about website planning, design, usability and emerging digital technology.

Website strategy

What makes a business website effective?

A useful business website explains what the business does, who it serves and what visitors can do next. It should organise information so visitors can understand the offering without unnecessary confusion.

Important considerations include clear messaging, appropriate navigation, relevant content, mobile usability and accessible contact information.

Redesign planning

When should you consider a redesign?

A website may need a new direction when its content no longer reflects the business, important information is difficult to find or the experience does not work comfortably on modern devices.

A redesign should begin with identifying actual problems rather than changing the appearance without a clear reason.

User experience

Why does navigation matter?

Navigation helps visitors understand where they are and where they can go. Clear menus, descriptive page names and consistent layouts reduce unnecessary effort.

The goal is to make the website easier to understand, not to add more menu options than necessary.

Responsive design

Why should businesses think beyond desktop?

Visitors use different devices. A responsive website considers how text, images, navigation and interactive elements behave at different screen sizes.

Testing on mobile and tablet is an important part of reviewing a website experience.

AI and websites

Where can AI fit into a website?

AI may support conversational assistance, content discovery and other visitor interactions. Its usefulness depends on the quality of information, the task it performs and how it is integrated.

A production AI assistant needs a secure backend, appropriate data handling and reliable service configuration.

Brand identity

Why is design consistency important?

Consistent colours, typography, imagery and tone help a website feel like one connected experience rather than a collection of unrelated pages.

A visual system also makes future content and page additions easier to organise.
