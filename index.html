06 / Questions

A few things
worth knowing.

What services does Northstar Digital provide?

We focus on website design, website redesign, responsive layouts and digital experience improvements.

Do you work with different industries?

Yes. We welcome enquiries from businesses across different industries and assess each project based on its goals and requirements.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. We can discuss your existing website, what you want to improve and the direction you have in mind.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Responsive design is part of our website approach. The final implementation should be tested across common screen sizes before launch.

How does the project process work?

We begin with an enquiry and discovery conversation, then define the scope, design direction, creation and review stages.

How much does a website cost?

Pricing depends on the pages, features, content and complexity involved. Contact us with your requirements to discuss a suitable scope.

How do I get started?

Use the project enquiry form below. Share a little about your business and what you would like to create.

Can I speak to the AI assistant?

Yes. Use the floating concierge icon to ask about our services, process or how to submit a project enquiry.
